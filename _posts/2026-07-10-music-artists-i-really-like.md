I want a public place where I can find artists I really like.
I’ve never been good at building and refining playlists.
What I like is to listen to full albums.
This place is my non-exhaustive chaotic and unstructured list.
It’s dynamic and will grow with time.
I’m using it to pick some random albums when I don’t know what to listen to.
Streaming apps are very good to recommend the most likely thing I want to listen to.
But sometimes I just want to go back to some old stuff statistics would never recommend.
